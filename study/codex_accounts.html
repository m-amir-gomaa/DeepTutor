How switching works

A running request keeps the account it started with. Your selection applies to the next request, including the agent’s next model call after a tool. The latest completed request above records the actual account used, including fallback.

Each account uses the chosen model and keeps its own limits. Fallback tries at most one other connected account after a rejection before any output. It does not replay a partial answer or repeat completed tools. No API-key billing fallback.
