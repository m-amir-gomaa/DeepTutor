replaying partial output. It incorporates upstream's protected custom headers,

Anki integration and generated-code isolation are retained. No personal books,
learner state, OAuth grants or provider keys were migrated or copied. DeepTutor
agent research changes remain deferred until the connected workflow, providers,
model choices and execution backends work together.
